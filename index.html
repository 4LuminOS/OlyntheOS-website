What We Build

01

Powered by Lumin

An integrated AI assistant based on Llama 3. Runs 100% locally — no cloud, no data leaks, no subscription. It helps you write, navigate, think, and work efficiently.

Lumin uses advanced natural language processing to understand your queries and provide intelligent responses. It helps with coding, writing, research, and tasks across our tools — while keeping your data private and secure on your machine.

The AI model is optimized for performance and runs efficiently on modern hardware, providing quick responses without cloud connectivity.

02

Privacy First

Zero telemetry by default. Your data never leaves local storage. No one scrutinizes it. You are the sole owner — Swiss philosophy applied to computing.

Our software has no tracking, no analytics, and no hidden data collection. Your files, conversations with Lumin, and usage patterns stay completely private.

Built with privacy-focused tools and open-source components you can audit yourself.

03

Open Source

Everything we build is free and open source. Transparent code, community-driven development, and no vendor lock-in. You own what you run.

All our projects are published under permissive licenses. You can inspect, modify, and redistribute the code as you see fit.

We believe in collaboration, transparency, and giving back to the community that supports us.

04

Built for Productivity

Practical tools designed for real work. Clean interfaces, thoughtful features, and everything you need to get things done efficiently.

Our software focuses on usability and performance. No bloat, no unnecessary complexity — just tools that work.

Regular updates and active development keep our projects fresh and functional.
